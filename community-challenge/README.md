# ThunderID Community Challenge

**Learn. Build. Share.**

The ThunderID Community Challenge is a community-led programme where developers explore modern identity topics, build working solutions with [ThunderID](https://thunderid.dev), and share their knowledge with the wider open-source community.

Each challenge focuses on a specific technical area, such as securing agentic applications, integrating with digital wallets, decentralized identity, cloud-native deployment, or securing modern applications and workloads.

- **Build the identity knowledge tomorrow requires.**
- **Walk away with valuable prizes from our sponsors.**
- **Take part in the modern open-source IAM community.**

The prize package is agreed for each challenge. A typical package is:

- **First place:** Around USD 1,500
- **Second place:** Around USD 1000
- **Third place:** Around USD 500
- **Best Community Voice:** Around USD 250

Exact prizes, eligibility, and payment arrangements are published with each challenge.

## How the programme works

1. **Learn:** Join a community-hosted challenge, learn about its technical topic and relevant ThunderID capabilities, and register for the active challenge.
2. **Build:** Develop a working project with ThunderID during the challenge period, which is usually two weeks.
3. **Share:** Publish your project, demonstrate what you built, and share what you learned. Qualifying projects are evaluated for prizes and recognition within the open-source community.

Challenges may be hosted physically, virtually, or in a hybrid format. Each challenge provides its own schedule, technical resources, submission requirements, evaluation criteria, and prize information.
