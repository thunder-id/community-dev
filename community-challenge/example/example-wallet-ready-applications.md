## Why this challenge matters

Digital wallets allow citizens to prove facts about themselves without repeatedly sharing complete identity documents. However, wallets create value only when applications and services can accept and verify the information they provide.

This challenge asks you to make a simple application wallet-ready. Your application will request selected information from a test wallet, use ThunderID to verify it, and complete a meaningful user journey.

You will not need to build a wallet or a complete digital-identity ecosystem. The programme will provide a test environment, sample credentials, technical guidance, and support.

Possible journeys include:

- Proving that someone meets an age requirement.
- Verifying student, resident, membership, or professional status.
- Completing registration or sign-in with a wallet credential.
- Granting access to an event, service, benefit, or application.

## What you can gain

By participating, you can:

- Understand the issuer-holder-verifier model.
- Gain practical experience with wallet-based credential presentation and verification.
- Explore consent, selective disclosure, and privacy-conscious application design.
- Learn how verified wallet information can drive application decisions.
- Receive guidance from ThunderID developers and maintainers.
- Create a public project and contribute useful knowledge to the open-source community.

You may work individually or as part of a small team. Your project should demonstrate one clear wallet-enabled user journey through a working implementation.
